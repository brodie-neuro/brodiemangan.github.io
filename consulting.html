Consulting & Services

I offer freelance consulting in EEG methodology, statistical analysis, research methods, and academic editing. All services are available remotely. Most work is asynchronous — you send your materials, I deliver within an agreed timeframe. Video consultations available on request.

EEG Methodology & Pipeline Consulting

I develop state-of-the-art EEG preprocessing and analysis pipelines. Services include: preprocessing pipeline design and implementation (artifact rejection, ICA, channel interpolation), time-frequency analysis (theta-gamma phase-amplitude coupling, specparam/FOOOF), electrode montage and reference scheme guidance, custom MATLAB/Python scripts, experimental design consultation, and pipeline audits.

Statistical Analysis & Consulting

Services include: linear mixed-effects models, power analysis and sample size planning, research design consultation, R and Python statistical programming, APA-formatted results and publication-ready figures.

Academic Proofreading & Editing

Dissertations, theses, and journal manuscripts — particularly in psychology and neuroscience. Proofreading and copy-editing, structural and clarity feedback, APA 7th edition formatting, and tailored feedback for ESL authors.

I also built ScholarRef, a free tool for automated citation style conversion in Word documents. See the ScholarRef page.

Research Methods Tutoring

Online tutoring for undergraduate and postgraduate students in research methods, statistics, and cognitive psychology.

Email me at hello@brodiemangan.com with a brief description of what you need. I'll get back to you within 48 hours with availability and a quote.
